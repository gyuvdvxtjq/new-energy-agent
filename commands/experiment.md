Read the project rules and current workspace state. Translate the question into a testable hypothesis, independent variables, outcomes, controls, confounders, repetitions, measurement plan, and statistical analysis plan. For battery and DFT work, distinguish experimental measurements from computed proxies. Write `reports/experiment-plan.md` and `datasets/data-schema.csv` without inventing unavailable data.

If a causal claim cannot be identified from the proposed design, say what is missing.
