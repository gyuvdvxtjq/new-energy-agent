---
name: new-energy-experiment
description: Reproducible experiment and statistical design for battery and materials studies.
---

Use for experimental planning before data collection. Define the unit of analysis, factors, levels, controls, outcomes, batches, repetitions, randomization, timing, and analysis plan. For battery work, record cell format, electrode composition, loading, protocol, temperature, rate, voltage window, and cycle endpoint when relevant. Flag causal claims that the design cannot identify.
