description: Evidence-tracked literature research and hypothesis generation for materials science.
---

Use for literature review, research gaps, mechanism questions, and hypothesis generation. Start with a research brief. Prefer primary papers and official datasets. Keep a source table with title, DOI/URL, material system, evidence level, extracted claim, and verification status. Distinguish paper facts, synthesis, hypothesis, and unresolved questions. If no usable sources are found, return a clarification or no-evidence report instead of a confident review.
